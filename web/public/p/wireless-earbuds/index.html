Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about buying from China with CrossBuy

Which platforms does CrossBuy search?

CrossBuy compares the same product across Taobao, Tmall, JD.com, Pinduoduo and 1688 in a single search, flagging the fastest, cheapest and best-value options.

What is included in the landed price?

Landed price = item price + international shipping + customs duty + VAT + service fee + payment fee, computed in real time under your destination country's tax rules and itemized before checkout — no hidden fees.

Which countries can CrossBuy deliver to?

We ship to the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Australia, Canada and more, with multiple carrier options and pre-order price and delivery estimates.

Can multiple orders ship together?

Yes. Items from different platforms are combined into one parcel, shipping is billed on combined weight, and duty-free allowances apply to the merged declared value — usually cheaper than separate shipments.

How long does delivery take?

Economy lines take about 5-10 business days, express lines about 3-7, depending on destination and carrier. Live estimates are shown before you order.

Why is CrossBuy cheaper?

China's domestic prices sit far below overseas retail markups. CrossBuy plugs you directly into source prices and makes shipping and taxes fully transparent, so you decide on the true landed total.

Which payment methods are supported?

PayPal, Visa, Mastercard and other major international payment methods are supported, with settlement in USD, EUR, GBP, JPY and 20+ currencies. Exchange rates and payment fees are shown transparently before checkout.

How do I track my parcel?

Once your order is picked up, an international tracking number is provided. You can follow both the domestic and cross-border legs in real time on the CrossBuy order page and the carrier's website until delivery.

Can I return or exchange items?

Quality checks are performed before dispatch. If a quality issue is found after delivery, you can request a return or exchange within the allowed window, and our support team helps negotiate with the marketplace seller.

Can I choose the service fee myself?

Yes. The service fee is buyer-selected (0%-30%, default 5%) and shown as a separate line item before checkout; a higher rate means higher service priority.
